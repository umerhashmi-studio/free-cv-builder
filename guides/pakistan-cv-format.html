Pakistan CV Format: What to Include for Private, Government and Overseas Jobs

Updated November 2026 · 7 minute read

CVs in Pakistan are in transition. The traditional "bio-data" style, with father's name, CNIC, religion, domicile and a photo, is still what many government and older organisations expect. Banks, telecom operators, multinationals, software houses and overseas employers want a shorter, skills-focused CV. Which one you write depends on where you are sending it.

The short version

  • Private sector: one or two pages. Personal details are limited to name, city, phone, email and LinkedIn.
  • Government jobs: follow the advertisement and the official application form exactly.
  • Do not put your CNIC number on a CV you post or email widely.
  • List your most recent education first, with CGPA or percentage.

Private-sector jobs

Corporate recruiters in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad skim CVs in seconds and many large employers collect applications through online portals. Keep to this order:

  1. Header. Name, city, mobile number in +92 format, a professional email address, LinkedIn.
  2. Summary. Two or three lines on your field, experience and strongest skill.
  3. Experience. Most recent first, with results in numbers where you have them.
  4. Education. Degree, university, year, CGPA.
  5. Skills and certifications.

Father's name, religion, marital status and full home address are not needed for these applications. A photo is optional: it is still common in sales, hospitality and front-desk roles and rarely included for IT, finance and multinational roles.

Government and public-sector jobs

Recruitment through FPSC, the provincial public service commissions and most government departments runs on prescribed application forms, not on your own CV design. Those forms ask for CNIC, father's name, date of birth, domicile and sometimes religion, because age limits and provincial or regional quotas are applied using them. Read the advertisement, use the exact form or portal it names, and attach the documents it lists. If a CV is requested in addition, include the same details so the two match.

Protect your CNIC

CVs get forwarded, uploaded to job groups and left on shared computers. A CNIC number together with your name, date of birth and address is enough for identity misuse, including SIM registration fraud. Provide it only on official application forms or when a genuine employer asks during hiring. The same goes for copies of your CNIC and educational documents.

Listing your education

Start with your highest qualification and work backwards:

  • University degrees. "BS Computer Science, University of the Punjab, 2025, CGPA 3.4/4.0". Always show what the CGPA is out of.
  • Intermediate and Matric. "FSc Pre-Engineering, BISE Lahore, 2021, 85%". If you have A-levels and O-levels, list subjects and grades.
  • Once you have a degree and some experience, Matric and Intermediate can shrink to one line each, or be dropped for private-sector applications.

For jobs or study abroad, employers and universities may ask for degree attestation from HEC. It is worth knowing the process before you need it at short notice.

Fresh graduates

With little or no work history, put education first and build the rest of the page from evidence of what you can do:

  • Final year project. What you built, the tools you used and your own part in it.
  • Internships. Even six weeks counts. Say what you actually did.
  • Freelance work. Real client projects are work experience. Describe them like a job.
  • Societies and volunteering. Include roles where you organised, led or handled money.
  • Courses and certifications. Only ones you completed.

One page is the right length. Skip "Objective" paragraphs about seeking a challenging position in a reputable organisation; a specific two-line summary does more.

Applying abroad or to remote jobs

Switch conventions to match the destination. For the Gulf, add nationality, visa status and a photo, as set out in our Gulf CV guide. For the US and UK, remove the photo and all personal details. For remote roles with foreign companies, use the US format, state your time zone, and link to a portfolio or GitHub profile.

Common mistakes

  • Sending the file as "CV.pdf" or "New Document (3).docx". Name it "Firstname-Lastname-CV.pdf".
  • An informal email address. Create a plain one with your name.
  • Listing every short course and seminar. Keep the ones relevant to the job.
  • Photographs of a printed CV. Always send a real PDF.
  • The same CV for every job. Adjust the summary and skills to each advert.
