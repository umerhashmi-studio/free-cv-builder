Frequently asked

Is the PDF download really free?

Yes — every template, unlimited downloads, no card required. We may run ads or add optional premium templates later, but the core builder and PDF export stay free.

Should I add a photo to my resume or CV?

In the US, Canada and most ATS-driven corporate roles, skip the photo — it isn't expected and some ATS systems can't parse it. In much of Europe, the Gulf region and parts of Asia, a photo is commonly included — use the Gulf CV or Modern template for that. Read our region guides for specifics.

What's the difference between a resume and a CV?

In the US, "resume" means a short (1-2 page) summary and "CV" means a long academic document. Everywhere else, "CV" is just the everyday word for what Americans call a resume. See our CV vs resume guide.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser's local storage only, on your own device. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, use the "Export backup" button in the builder first to save a copy you can re-import later.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the builder and start — nothing to sign up for.
