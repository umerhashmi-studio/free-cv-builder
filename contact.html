What to include

What we cannot help with

Your CV is stored only on your own device, so we have no copy and cannot recover one that was lost when browser data was cleared. Use Export backup in the builder to keep a file you control.

We do not offer jobs, visas or recruitment services, and we will never ask you for money. If someone contacts you claiming to do so in our name, it is not us.
