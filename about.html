About this project

We checked what the big resume builders — Zety, Novorésumé, Canva — actually charge before building this tool. The pattern was the same everywhere: design your resume for free, then get stopped at the final step by a paywall, a trial card, or a format downgrade (plain text instead of a real PDF).

This tool exists on one idea: nobody should have to pay just to download the PDF of their own resume. Every template, every section, and the PDF export itself are free, with no signup and no limit on how many times you download.

It's built to work for whichever format you need — a short US-style resume, a UK/EU-style CV, or a Gulf-region CV with a photo and visa-status fields — because "resume" and "CV" mean the same everyday document almost everywhere outside the US, and job seekers shouldn't need five different tools to cover that.

How it works, technically

Everything runs in your browser. Your details are saved to your own device's local storage — not uploaded to a server — and the PDF is generated using your browser's own print function, not a third-party conversion service. See our privacy policy for details.
